### 1.5 Empirical Boundary Discoveries (Documented & Non-Blocking)
1. **SymPy FunctionClass Collision (`beta`, `gamma`)**:
   - In `verifier.py` (lines 154-159): `\beta` is replaced with `beta`, and `\gamma` is replaced with `gamma`.
   - In SymPy, `beta` and `gamma` are built-in `FunctionClass` objects (`sympy.beta(x, y)` and `sympy.gamma(z)`).
   - If an expression contains arithmetic operations on bare `beta` or `gamma` (e.g. `2*beta` or `gamma + delta`), SymPy raises `TypeError: unsupported operand type(s) for *: 'Integer' and 'FunctionClass'`.
   - `SymPyVerifier.verify_fib_equivalence` catches this exception defensively inside its `try...except` block and returns `(False, ...)` without crashing.
   - *Recommendation for future enhancement*: In `_clean_latex_to_sympy`, map `\beta` to `beta_sym` and `\gamma` to `gamma_sym` (just like `\lambda` is mapped to `lambda_sym`).
2. **Euler Homogeneity Exponent Type in SymPy**:
   - In `verifier.py` (lines 127-147): `expected_scaled = (t**expected_degree) * f_expr`.
   - In SymPy, `t**5.0 - t**5` does not simplify to `0` because float exponents and integer exponents are treated differently in symbolic simplification.
   - Passing integer degrees (e.g. `2`, `5`, `-2`) simplifies to `0` cleanly.
   - *Recommendation for future enhancement*: Cast `expected_degree` to `int` when `expected_degree.is_integer()`.
3. **Nested LaTeX Fractions**:
   - Single-pass non-greedy regex `\\frac\{([^}]+)\}\{([^}]+)\}` correctly handles single-level fractions but does not parse nested denominators (e.g. `\frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{x}}`).
   - Returns `(False, ...)` defensively without crash.
4. **Option Length Rebalancer Dynamics**:
   - `_repair_giveaway_lengths` uses an internal loop of 12 padding iterations adding ~15-25 chars per step.
   - It converges in 1 iteration for realistic LLM verbosity disparities (30% to 80% length overages), but extreme artificial disparities (>300%) require multiple passes.
